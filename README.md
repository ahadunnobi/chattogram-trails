# Chattogram Trekking Trails Guide

Chattogram features **diverse hiking trails** ranging from steep peak climbs to slippery, waterfall-laden jungle treks. The major trekking routes are primarily distributed across Sitakund, Mirsarai, and the deep Chittagong Hill Tracts region. 

## Waterfall & Stream Trails (Mirsarai & Sitakund)
These trails follow natural water streams (**jhiri**) and require navigating rocky, slippery terrain and wading through shallow water.

* **Napittachhara Trail:** Starts from Nayaduar Bazar along the Dhaka–Chittagong Highway. This relatively easy trail rewards trekkers with three distinct waterfalls and natural swimming pools. It is highly popular during the rainy season. View details on the [Napittachhara Trail Map](https://google.com).
* **Harimmala Hatbanga Trail:** A challenging 12 km wilderness trek taking roughly 6–7 hours to complete. The path starts at Harimmala village and moves through dense forests, crossing several streams before ending at Hatubanga village.

## Summit & Ridge Hikes
These trails prioritize vertical elevation gain, leading hikers up the steep ridges of the region to historic temples or panoramic viewpoints.

* **Chandranath Hill Trail:** A steep, iconic climb involving a mix of earthen paths and hundreds of stairs leading to the sacred [Chandranath Temple](https://google.com). It is accessible through the **Botanical Garden & Eco-Park** side for a more gradual, paved ascent.
* **Keokradong Peak Trail:** Located further out in the Bandarban Hill Tracts, this multi-day trek leads to one of Bangladesh's highest recognized peaks. The journey features dramatic valley vistas, stops at Boga Lake, and encounters with local indigenous villages.

## University & Campus Trails
Located closer to the city center, these trails offer quicker, semi-guided hikes on soft dirt tracks through rolling hills.

* **Chalanda Giripath:** A short, high-thrill 30 to 40-minute canyon trail tucked inside the Chittagong University hills. Hikers navigate a narrow, deep earthen ravine that requires caution but delivers great geological views. Plan using the [Chalanda Giripath Guide](https://google.com).
* **Nira Pahar:** An easy hill-hopping walking track situated behind the Farhad Hossain Hall at Chittagong University. A network of interconnected ridges makes it highly customizable for duration, but climbing during the heavy monsoon is discouraged. Navigation details are on the [Nira Pahar Map](https://google.com).

## Essential Trekking Safety
* **Leech & Footwear Protection:** Stream trails are heavily infested with leeches during wet months. Wear long socks, snug trekking shoes, and carry salt or anti-leech spray.
* **Slippery Rocks:** Waterfall bases contain moss-covered rocks that cause severe falls. Tread with extreme care.
* **Permits for Hill Tracts:** Traveling deep into Bandarban or Rangamati routes requires official security permits and military checkpoint log-ins. Foreign travelers must apply well in advance through local authorized tour agencies.
